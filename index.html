常见问题

怎么让判定更准？
把手机放在离鼓垫一臂远的地方；预备拍那一小节先别敲，让手机听清节拍器；戴耳机听节拍器效果最好。

怎么解锁更快的速度？
在当前上限速度达标两次（A 级，准确又稳定），就会开放快 4 BPM 的下一档。100 BPM 以内的速度阶梯和全部节奏徽章都免费。

完整版包含什么？
速度阶梯一路开放到 240 BPM、全部 8 种基础手法与自由组合、保存录音回放、完整的进步历史。完整版是一次性买断，不是订阅。

换手机后怎么恢复完整版？
用同一个 Apple 账号登录，在 App 的「设置」里点「恢复购买」。

Wenpai — Support (English)

Wenpai (“steady beat”) is a timing coach for drummers: play along with the click and your phone tells you whether each stroke was early, on, or late.

Contact. Questions, feedback on a verdict, or feature ideas: open an issue here.

More accurate verdicts. Keep the phone an arm's length from the pad, sit out the count-in bar so the phone can hear the click, and use headphones for the most precise timing.

Unlocking faster tempos. Two qualifying runs (an A grade, accurate and steady) at your current limit open the next step, 4 BPM faster. The tempo ladder up to 100 BPM and all rhythm badges are free.

Full Version. A one-time purchase, not a subscription: the ladder all the way to 240 BPM, all 8 rudiments with free combinations, saved recordings and your full progress history. On a new phone, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in Settings.
